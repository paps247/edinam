About Super Strong 42.5R

Product Overview

Ghacem Super Strong 42.5R is a Portland-limestone cement designed for structural concrete applications where high early strength, consistent quality and dependable performance are required.

Key Features

  • High Early Strength
  • Excellent Workability
  • Reliable Quality
  • Suitable for Structural Construction
  • Manufactured by Ghacem Ltd.

📦 Storage Guide

  • Store cement in a dry place.
  • Keep bags off the ground.
  • Protect from moisture.
  • Stack bags properly.
  • Use older bags first; First In, First Out (FIFO).

🦺 Safety Information

  • Wear gloves when handling cement.
  • Wear eye protection.
  • Avoid inhaling cement dust.
  • Wash skin after contact.
  • Rinse eyes immediately if exposed.
